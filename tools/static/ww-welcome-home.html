Welcome Home · structure before style

One narrow centered column for a phone. Its whole job: make a stranger feel personally expected by a specific human, fast, and get them to RSVP to the next orientation. Grey-box and structural only - layout, hierarchy, content order, and states. The region order is the argument; do not reorder it.

Echo surface. Submitting the form writes this person's name and email - phone is optional - to their one Echo Person record, never to Action Network. No login is ever required here. Known people are read in from Action Network by email; this page only confirms, it never writes dues.

No per-person fight data. We do not know why this individual landed here, so every "fight" reference is a shared, collective framing - the common squeeze of rent, utilities, and the cost of getting by, phrased as "us," never "your." Exception: if a QR or link is tied to a specific campaign, that campaign's fight is fair game - it lives on the link, not on the person.
