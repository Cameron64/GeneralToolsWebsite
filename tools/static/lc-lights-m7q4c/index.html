The fix

A small database the chapter owns.

Managed, encrypted, backed up daily - and every read is logged. Inside it: names, contact details, join dates, dues status. The monthly export already arrives with those fields; today we strip them out before anything lands in Drive, because a spreadsheet is no place to keep them. This is.

It is the sign-in book we already keep at every meeting - except this one also writes down who opened it.

The same idea, as a diagram. The monthly export lands in a store we own, and Echo - the chapter's own app - is how anyone touches it: role-based access, every read logged. Echo adds what it learns, too - event sign-ins, link clicks - so the picture gets richer the more we use it.

Not new scope

We already asked for all of this.

  1. ✓Member rolls, event logs, access that rotates - the Neighborhood Groups resolution asks for them in those words. We drew the four neighborhoods in May.
  2. ✓A secure, maintainable home for member data - a goal already written into our own tech plans.

The resolution passed. The goal is written. The question is already worked by hand. All that is missing is the database - and a named owner.

What I want to do

Three steps, one at a time.

  1. 1Stand up the infrastructure - the database, plus a real home for Echo, the chapter's app: managed, encrypted, access-logged, chapter-owned.
  2. 2Load what already arrives - load the monthly Action Network export instead of hand-copying it, sorted into the four neighborhoods. No new data collected.
  3. 3Build the screens together - they come last, shaped by what this room needs to see.

Each step is small on its own, and each is reversible.

By neighborhood, where people live. By cohort, over time. One count tells you a number - two counts tell you a direction. And the tool keeps nobody - organizing does that. It just shows who is drifting while there is still time to knock on a door.

The opposite of surveillance

Surveillance is being watched without knowing it. This is our own membership data, in our own hands: off Google, seen by fewer people than today, every look logged. No AI in the product - nothing predicts, nothing profiles, nothing watches.
